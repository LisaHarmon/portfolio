# Quick Start Guide
---

After you [install and set up Smart Shopper]({% link docs/samples/software/user_guide_install_setup_ss.md %}), start using the app with these steps:

<details markdown="1" style="margin-top: 1rem;">

  <summary><strong>Create your first shopping list.</strong></summary>

1. Tap **Lists**.
2. Select **New List**, enter your list name, and then tap **Create**.
3. When the new list appears, tap **Add an item**.
4. Search for the item, or use the barcode scanner.
5. Tap the item you want to add. Your list appears and displays the item you added.

</details>

<details markdown="1" style="margin-top: 1rem;">

  <summary><strong>Compare prices.</strong></summary>

1. Tap **Compare Stores**, and then select the shopping list you want to use. A list of available stores appears.
2. Select up to three stores, and then tap **Continue**. A grid appears and displays your items, each item's price at your chosen stores, and each store total for your list. 

    > If an **x** appears for the price, the item is out of stock at that store.

3. Review the item details and store totals to choose the best store.

</details>

<details markdown="1" style="margin-top: 1rem;">

  <summary><strong>Use the store map.</strong></summary>

1. When you arrive at the store, go to the list you want to shop.
2. Tap **Store Map**. An interactive store map appears, showing a highlighted route and the location of each list item.
3. Follow the highlighted route through the store aisles.

</details>
